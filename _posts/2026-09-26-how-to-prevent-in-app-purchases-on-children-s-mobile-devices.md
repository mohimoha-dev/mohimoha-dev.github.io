Free games often aren't free. They're designed to sell gems, coins, skins and "just one more life". Children can rack up large bills in minutes if purchases aren't locked down. Here's how to prevent in-app purchases on Android and iPhone, and what to do if it already happened.

## Android: require authentication for purchases

On the child's device (or any shared device):

1. Open the **Google Play Store** → profile picture → **Settings → Authentication**.
2. Tap **Require authentication for purchases**.
3. Choose **For all purchases through Google Play on this device**.

Now every purchase requires the account password or biometrics.

## Android: use Google Family Link (best for kids)

1. Install **Family Link** on your phone and set up the child's Google account.
2. Go to **Controls → Content restrictions → Google Play → Purchases & download approvals**.
3. Choose **All content** (or **Only paid content**) to require your approval.
4. Also set **age-based ratings** for apps and games.

Your child can request purchases; you approve or deny from your phone.

## Remove saved payment methods

On a child's or shared Android device:

- **Play Store → Payments & subscriptions → Payment methods** → remove cards, or use **gift card balances** only.

## iPhone and iPad: Ask to Buy

With **Family Sharing**:

1. On your iPhone: **Settings → [your name] → Family Sharing → [child's name] → Ask to Buy** → turn on.
2. Any purchase or free download request comes to you for approval.

## iPhone: Screen Time restrictions

1. **Settings → Screen Time → Content & Privacy Restrictions** → turn on.
2. **iTunes & App Store Purchases → In-app Purchases → Don't Allow**.
3. Set **Require Password → Always Require**.
4. Set a **Screen Time passcode** different from the device passcode.

## Other tips

- Don't share your **device passcode** or **Face ID** with young children.
- Turn off **"Remember password"** or biometric purchases on shared devices.
- Review **subscriptions** regularly (Play Store and App Store) for accidental sign-ups.
- Teach kids about **"free" games and ads**.

## If purchases already happened

- **Google Play:** go to play.google.com → **Order history** → **Request a refund**. Accidental purchases by children are often refunded, especially if reported quickly.
- **Apple:** visit **reportaproblem.apple.com** and select the purchase.
- Contact your **bank** if charges were unauthorised.
