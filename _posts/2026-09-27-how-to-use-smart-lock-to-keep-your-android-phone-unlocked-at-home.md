Entering your PIN dozens of times a day at home gets tiring. Android's **Smart Lock**, now called **Extend Unlock** on newer versions, keeps your phone unlocked in trusted situations: at home, connected to your smartwatch, or while it's in your pocket.

## Where to find it

- **Pixel / stock Android:** Settings → Security & privacy → **More security & privacy → Extend Unlock** (or **Smart Lock** on older versions).
- **Samsung:** Settings → Lock screen and AOD → **Extend Unlock / Smart Lock**.

You'll need a screen lock (PIN, pattern or password) set first.

## The options

### 1. On-body detection

Keeps the phone unlocked **after you unlock it while carrying it** (in your hand or pocket). It locks when you put it down.

- **Caution:** it can't tell *who* is carrying the phone. If it's snatched while unlocked, it may stay unlocked.

### 2. Trusted places

Keeps the phone unlocked at locations you choose (e.g., **home**), using location services.

- Location accuracy varies; the "trusted" area can extend to neighbours or the street.
- Requires location to be on.

### 3. Trusted devices

Keeps the phone unlocked while connected via Bluetooth to a device you trust, such as a **smartwatch, car stereo or earbuds**.

- Best with **smartwatches** you always wear.
- Bluetooth range can reach 10m or more, so someone nearby could use your phone.

## Security trade-offs

Extend Unlock trades security for convenience. Recommendations:

- **Use trusted devices** (watch) rather than trusted places for better control.
- Avoid **on-body detection** if you often use public transport.
- Don't enable it on phones with **work profiles** or sensitive data if your employer prohibits it.
- Android periodically **requires your PIN** anyway (for example, every 4 hours of inactivity or 72 hours), which limits risk.

## Alternatives that stay secure

- **Face or fingerprint unlock** is fast and secure.
- **Longer screen timeout at home** (with a quick lock when leaving).
- **Watch Unlock** features on some phones, which require the watch to be unlocked on your wrist.

## Troubleshooting

- Not working: check **location and Bluetooth** permissions, battery optimisation for Google Play services, and update the phone.
- Trusted place not detected: re-add it or set a custom address.
