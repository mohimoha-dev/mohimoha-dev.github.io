Your laptop downloads at full speed, but your phone crawls on the same Wi-Fi. Or everything was fine until last week. Slow phone Wi-Fi usually comes down to signal, band, interference or a phone setting, and most causes can be fixed in a few minutes.

## Step 1: Measure properly

- Run a speed test on the phone **next to the router**, then in the room where it's slow.
- Compare with another device in the same spot.

If it's fast near the router but slow elsewhere, it's **coverage**. If it's slow everywhere, it's a **phone or router setting**.

## Step 2: Use the right band

- **5GHz / 6GHz:** faster, less interference, shorter range.
- **2.4GHz:** longer range but slower and crowded.

If your router uses separate network names, connect to the **5GHz** one when you're nearby. If it uses band steering (one name), move closer so the phone chooses 5GHz.

## Step 3: Improve router placement

- Place the router **centrally and high**, not in a cupboard or behind the TV.
- Avoid **metal, fish tanks and thick walls** between router and phone.
- Consider a **mesh system** or access point for large homes.

## Step 4: Restart and forget the network

- Restart the phone and router.
- **Forget** the Wi-Fi network and reconnect.

## Step 5: Check phone settings

- **VPN:** turn it off and test; VPNs often cut speed.
- **Private DNS:** set to **Automatic** temporarily (Android) to rule out a slow DNS provider.
- **Data Saver / Low Data Mode:** can throttle background downloads.
- **Battery saver / Low Power Mode:** can reduce Wi-Fi performance.
- **"Wi-Fi assist" / "Switch to mobile data":** may make the phone hop networks.
- **MAC randomisation / Private Wi-Fi address:** rarely, some routers handle it badly; try disabling for your home network.

## Step 6: Router settings

- **Update the router firmware.**
- Change the **Wi-Fi channel** (use a Wi-Fi analyser app to find a less crowded one).
- Use **WPA2/WPA3**, not old WEP/WPA (which force slow speeds).
- Enable **Wi-Fi 6/6E** if your phone and router support it.
- Check for **bandwidth hogs**: other devices streaming or downloading.

## Step 7: Check the case and the phone

- Thick metal or magnetic cases can weaken reception.
- If the phone was dropped or repaired, the Wi-Fi antenna may be damaged.
- **Reset network settings** as a last software step.

Test near and far from the router, switch to 5GHz, place the router well, disable VPN and power saving, update firmware and choose a clear channel. Most slow phone Wi-Fi problems disappear with these steps.
