Sending a 4K video through WhatsApp or email turns crisp footage into a blurry mess, because those apps compress aggressively. If you want the **original file** on your computer, for editing, backup or archiving, you need a transfer method that copies it untouched.

## Why messaging apps ruin video quality

WhatsApp, Messenger, Telegram (by default), and email often **re-encode** video to a lower bitrate and resolution to save bandwidth. The result: smaller files and visible quality loss. The methods below copy the exact original file.

## Android → Windows

### USB cable (fastest and simplest)

1. Connect the phone with a **USB-C data cable**.
2. On the phone, tap the USB notification and choose **File transfer / Android Auto**.
3. On Windows, open **This PC → [Your phone] → Internal storage → DCIM → Camera**.
4. Copy the videos to your PC.

**Tip:** use a USB 3.x cable and port on phones that support it for much faster transfers.

### Quick Share (Nearby Share) for Windows

Install **Quick Share for Windows** from Google, then share videos from the phone's gallery wirelessly at full quality.

### Phone Link

Windows **Phone Link** can access recent photos, but for large videos, USB or Quick Share is more reliable.

## Android → Mac

- Use **OpenMTP** or **Android File Transfer** alternatives over USB (macOS doesn't support Android MTP natively).
- Or share over **Quick Share alternatives**, **LocalSend** (free, open source, Wi-Fi), or a cloud drive.

## iPhone → Mac

- **AirDrop:** select the video in Photos → Share → AirDrop → your Mac. Tap **Options → All Photos Data** to keep original format and metadata.
- **Image Capture** or **Photos app** over USB for bulk imports.

## iPhone → Windows

1. Connect via USB and tap **Trust This Computer**.
2. Open the **Photos app** on Windows → **Import**, or browse **This PC → Apple iPhone → Internal Storage → DCIM**.
3. On iPhone, set **Settings → Apps → Photos → Transfer to Mac or PC → Keep Originals** to avoid automatic conversion from HEVC.

## Wireless options that keep full quality

- **LocalSend:** free app for Android, iPhone, Windows, Mac and Linux over local Wi-Fi.
- **Google Drive / OneDrive / iCloud Drive:** upload originals and download on the computer (check the app's upload quality setting).
- **Telegram "Send as file":** sends without compression (unlike normal video sending).

## Speed comparison

| Method | Speed | Keeps original |
|---|---|---|
| USB 3.x cable | Very fast | ✅ |
| USB 2.0 cable | Moderate | ✅ |
| AirDrop / Quick Share | Fast | ✅ |
| LocalSend | Fast (Wi-Fi) | ✅ |
| Cloud drive | Depends on internet | ✅ |
| WhatsApp / email | Slow-moderate | ❌ Compressed |

## Tips

- Check your phone supports **USB 3** if you move huge files often.
- Use **exFAT-formatted** external drives for files over 4GB.
- Keep the phone **unlocked** during USB transfers on iPhone.
