Bluetooth trackers have become one of the most useful accessories you can own: clip one to your keys, drop one in your bag or luggage, and your phone can find it anywhere, even on the other side of the world. But the "best" tracker depends almost entirely on **which phone you use**, because each one relies on its own crowd-sourced network.

## How item trackers work

A tracker is a small Bluetooth beacon. When it's near your phone, you can make it ring. When it's far away, **other people's phones** on the same network detect it anonymously and report its location to you. The bigger the network, the better the chance of finding a lost item.

## The main options

### Apple AirTag

- **Works with:** iPhone and iPad only (setup requires an Apple device).
- **Network:** Apple's **Find My**, the largest tracking network, with every nearby iPhone helping.
- **Precision Finding:** Ultra-Wideband (UWB) arrows guide you to within centimetres on iPhone 11 and newer.
- **Battery:** replaceable CR2032 coin cell, about a year.
- **Downsides:** no keyring hole, so you need a holder. Useless for Android users.

### Samsung Galaxy SmartTag2

- **Works with:** Samsung Galaxy phones only (via SmartThings Find).
- **Network:** **SmartThings Find**, powered by Galaxy devices. Strong in regions where Samsung is popular.
- **Precision:** UWB directional finding on UWB-equipped Galaxy phones (typically Ultra and some Plus models).
- **Battery:** up to ~500 days in normal mode, longer in power-saving mode.
- **Extras:** built-in keyring loop, can act as a smart-home button.

### Google Find My Device network trackers (Chipolo, Pebblebee, Motorola, etc.)

- **Works with:** most Android phones (Android 9+ with Google Play services).
- **Network:** Google's **Find My Device** network, built from Android phones worldwide, huge in size but relying on nearby devices to be opted in.
- **Precision:** mostly Bluetooth ring-and-search; UWB support is arriving on some trackers and phones.
- **Options:** many shapes, including card-style trackers for wallets and rechargeable models.

### Cross-platform trackers

Some trackers (e.g., certain Chipolo and Pebblebee models) come in separate **Apple Find My** or **Google Find My Device** versions, and a few now support both. Check carefully before buying.

## Anti-stalking protections

All major networks now alert people if an **unknown tracker is travelling with them**:

- iPhone and Android both show **unknown tracker alerts** across platforms.
- Trackers play a sound when separated from their owner for a while.
- You can scan manually: Android **Settings → Safety & emergency → Unknown tracker alerts → Scan now**.

## Which should you buy?

- **iPhone user:** AirTag is the easy choice, with the best network and precision.
- **Samsung Galaxy user:** SmartTag2 for UWB precision and deep integration, or a Google-network tracker for the widest Android coverage.
- **Other Android phones:** a **Google Find My Device** tracker such as Chipolo or Pebblebee.
- **Mixed-phone household:** consider trackers that support both networks, or buy per-person.
