A second screen boosts productivity, and you might already have one in your pocket. With the right app, your phone can act as an **extra monitor** for your PC or Mac: perfect for chat windows, music controls, system monitors or reference documents.

## How it works

Software on the computer creates a **virtual display** and streams it to an app on your phone over **Wi-Fi or USB**. Your computer treats the phone like a real monitor that you can extend or mirror.

## Option 1: Spacedesk (Windows, free)

1. Install the **Spacedesk driver** on your Windows PC.
2. Install the **Spacedesk app** on your Android phone or iPhone.
3. Connect both to the **same Wi-Fi** (or use USB tethering).
4. Open the app and select your PC. The phone becomes an extended display.
5. Arrange the display position in **Windows Settings → System → Display**.

## Option 2: Duet Display (Windows and Mac, paid)

- Works over **USB and Wi-Fi**, low latency, supports touch input and pen on tablets.
- Developed by ex-Apple engineers; polished and reliable.

## Option 3: Splashtop Wired XDisplay

- **USB-based**, low latency, sharp image, good for Mac and Windows.

## Option 4: Mac users with iPhone/iPad

- **Sidecar** works with iPads, not iPhones.
- Third-party apps (Duet, etc.) support iPhones as extra displays.

## Wired vs wireless

| | USB | Wi-Fi |
|---|---|---|
| Latency | Lower | Higher |
| Stability | Better | Depends on network |
| Charging | Phone charges | Battery drains |
| Convenience | Cable needed | Cable-free |

## Best uses for a phone display

- **Chat apps** (Slack, Teams, Discord)
- **Music or video controls**
- **System monitors** (CPU, temperatures)
- **Reference notes** or documentation
- **Streaming dashboards** (OBS chat, alerts)

## Performance tips

- Use **5GHz Wi-Fi** or USB.
- Lower **resolution/quality** in the app if laggy.
- Keep the phone **plugged in** for long sessions.
- Use a **phone stand** at eye level.
- Enable **Do Not Disturb** on the phone to avoid notifications popping over the display.
