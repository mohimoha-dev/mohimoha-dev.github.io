A cheap charger or cable might seem like a bargain, but counterfeit and uncertified accessories are a leading cause of **damaged charging ports, slow charging, swollen batteries and even fires**. Here's how to tell genuine, safe chargers from dangerous fakes.

## Why fake chargers are dangerous

Genuine chargers contain carefully designed circuits that:

- **Isolate** mains voltage from the output
- **Regulate** voltage and current precisely
- **Protect** against overheating, short circuits and surges

Counterfeits often cut corners with **thin insulation, missing fuses and poor transformers**. Failures can deliver unstable power, damage your phone's charging circuit, or overheat and catch fire.

## Signs of a fake or unsafe charger

1. **Too light:** genuine chargers have substantial internal components.
2. **Poor printing:** blurry text, misspellings, wrong fonts, missing specs.
3. **Missing certification marks** for your region (e.g., **CE, UKCA, UL, ETL, FCC, BIS, RCM**), or marks that look wrong.
4. **Unrealistically cheap** price for a "branded" product.
5. **Loose or misaligned** prongs and seams.
6. **No brand or model number.**
7. **Gets very hot** or smells of burning plastic.
8. **Buzzing or crackling** sounds.

## Signs of a fake or poor cable

- **Thin, flimsy cable** and loose connector housing
- **Slow charging** or constant disconnects
- For iPhone Lightning cables: missing **MFi certification** (causes "accessory not supported")
- For USB-C high-wattage cables: missing **e-marker** (needed above 60W/3A)
- Connector feels **warm** during use

## How to buy safely

- Buy from **official brand stores** or **authorised retailers**.
- Choose reputable accessory brands (Anker, Belkin, Ugreen, Baseus, Spigen and similar).
- Check **reviews** for overheating complaints.
- Match the **protocol** (USB PD/PPS) and wattage to your phone.
- Avoid unbranded market-stall chargers.

## Warning signs during use: stop immediately if

- The charger or cable becomes **too hot to hold**
- You see **scorch marks**, melted plastic or sparks
- The phone shows **"charger not supported"** or moisture/overheating warnings repeatedly
- The battery starts **swelling**
