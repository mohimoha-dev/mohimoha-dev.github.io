You don't need a scanner, or a paid scanning app, to turn paper into clean PDFs. Both Android and iPhone include excellent built-in document scanners that detect edges, straighten pages, remove shadows and make text searchable.

## iPhone: Notes and Files

**Notes app:**

1. Open **Notes** → create a note → tap the **camera/paperclip icon** → **Scan Documents**.
2. Hold the phone over the document; it captures automatically when edges are detected.
3. Adjust corners if needed, add more pages, then **Save**.
4. Share as **PDF** from the note.

**Files app:** tap **⋯ → Scan Documents** to save directly as a PDF in iCloud Drive or on the phone.

**Live Text** lets you copy text from scans and photos.

## Android: Google Drive scanner

1. Open **Google Drive** → tap **+** (or the camera icon) → **Scan**.
2. Capture pages; Drive auto-crops and enhances.
3. Adjust **crop, colour** and add pages.
4. Save as a **PDF** to Drive. Scans become **searchable** with OCR.

## Samsung camera and Samsung Notes

- The **Samsung Camera** shows a "Scan document" prompt when it detects paper (enable **Scan documents and text** in camera settings).
- **Samsung Notes** also has a scan option that saves into notes.

## Google Lens and Pixel

- **Google Lens:** capture text, translate, or copy it to your computer.
- **Pixel camera / Files by Google** include document modes on many models.

## Tips for sharp, readable scans

1. Use **even light**. Avoid shadows from your hand or phone.
2. Place documents on a **contrasting, dark surface**.
3. Hold the phone **parallel** to the page.
4. Use **black & white** mode for text, **colour** for receipts and IDs.
5. Clean the **camera lens**.
6. For multi-page documents, scan in one session and **reorder** before saving.

## Organise and secure scans

- Name files clearly (e.g., "2026-09 Electricity bill").
- Store in **Drive, iCloud or OneDrive** folders.
- For sensitive documents (IDs, contracts), use a **Locked Folder, Secure Folder** or password-protected storage.
