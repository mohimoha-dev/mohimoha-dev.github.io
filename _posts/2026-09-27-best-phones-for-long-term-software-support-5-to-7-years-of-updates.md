A phone's hardware can last many years, but without software updates it becomes unsafe for banking, loses app compatibility, and drops in resale value. The good news: several brands now promise **five to seven years** of updates. Here's how to take advantage of that.

## Why long-term updates matter

- **Security patches** close vulnerabilities attackers actively exploit.
- **App compatibility:** banking, payment and work apps drop older OS versions.
- **New features** extend the phone's useful life.
- **Resale value** stays higher for phones with support remaining.
- **Lower cost per year:** a phone kept for six years can cost less annually than a cheaper one replaced every two.

## Brands with the longest commitments

Policies change and vary by model and region, so always confirm on the manufacturer's page. Broadly:

- **Google Pixel:** recent models promise **7 years** of OS and security updates, including recent a-series phones.
- **Samsung Galaxy:** recent S, Z and some A-series models promise up to **7 years**; cheaper A-series models may get fewer.
- **Apple iPhone:** historically **6–7+ years** of iOS updates, with minimum security support periods now stated.
- **OnePlus, Xiaomi, OPPO, vivo, Honor, Nothing:** flagships commonly **4–6 years** of security updates and several OS upgrades; budget models less.
- **Fairphone:** focuses on longevity with long support and repairable designs.

## How to calculate remaining support

Support usually counts from the **launch date**, not your purchase date.

**Example:** a phone launched in January 2025 with 7 years of updates is supported until roughly January 2032. Buy it in 2027 and you still get about five years.

Check:

1. The phone's **launch date**
2. The brand's **policy page** for that exact model
3. The current **security patch level** on the device (Settings → About phone / Security)

## Buying strategy

- **Keep phones 4+ years?** Choose a model with 6–7 years of promised updates.
- **Buying last year's flagship at a discount?** Great value if it still has 5+ years left.
- **Budget phone?** Prioritise brands and models with at least 4 years of security updates.

## Keep your phone updated

- Turn on **automatic updates** and install security patches promptly.
- Update apps through the Play Store/App Store.
- Replace the **battery** mid-life to enjoy the full support window.
